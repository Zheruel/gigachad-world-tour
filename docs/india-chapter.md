# The India chapter

The campaign begins with **The Night Train**, continues through **Dirty Delhi**, and ultimately reaches **Refund Tower**. The train is being rebuilt from its alpha version; Dirty Delhi exists and Refund Tower remains a design proposal.

| Level | Route | Design |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The Night Train | Remote night station → sleeper train → polluted city at dawn → river escape | [Approved fresh rebuild](night-train.md) |
| 2 · Dirty Delhi | Market streets → river district → dredging operation | [Existing level design](stage1-dirty-delhi.md) |
| 3 · Refund Tower | Monsoon streets → corporate tower → rooftop | [Proposed level design](stage3-refund-tower.md) |

CHAD is a wandering, absurdly confident action hero. Keep his blond flat-top, sunglasses, open leather vest, jeans and boots consistent. Comedy comes from confident action and escalating spectacle. Voice samples are sparse punctuation; no text-to-speech.

Shared combat retains movement, jumping, dashing, attacks, grabs, parries and supers. Each level introduces new situations through enemy composition and environment, rather than merely raising health. Relics, scores and unlocked levels remain persistent across visual rebuilds.

The train's old ticket theft, TTE, Birju and alpha asset reuse prescriptions are superseded by its approved replacement document. Its river escape establishes the next destination; further changes to Dirty Delhi are outside the train rebuild.
