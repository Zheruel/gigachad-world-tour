# LEVEL 1 — THE NIGHT TRAIN

Status: route, scenery, intro and finale implemented. The enemy cast, both boss fights and grab removal below are an approved redesign, not yet implemented. ## Journey

A 12–15 minute journey from a remote station at midnight through increasingly comfortable sleeper coaches into the industrial city at dawn. CHAD fights toward the restricted rear carriage, where a corrupt politician travels behind his security. No old alpha train artwork is retained.

## Combat

Shared guard reduces frontal ordinary damage by 90%; a fresh press opens a forgiving 12-tick parry window. Holding guard cannot repeatedly parry. Every parry grants a protected 45-tick punish and a 60-tick empowered follow-up. Jump and the existing double-tap dash avoid rare red specials. The single-target boxing rush locks one nearby enemy, never damages bystanders, and preserves sound 26 plus short Duke quotes. Four authored combinations vary the rhythm and finish while preserving 36 total damage, or 18 against intact boss guard. See combat impact below for knockdown and daze outcomes.

Seven families, one job and one answer each, one new face per area (attack colour: plain = guard it, or a fresh timed tap (not a re-tap inside an open window) deflects it: no damage, a steel glint, a little meter and a 12-tick recoil that ends his string, but no riposte or slow-motion; boss strings play on through a deflect, and a plain projectile would be absorbed, not returned. Green = parry, reflect = parry sends it back, red = sidestep or jump). Target: firm but fair; mashing is punished, reading and spacing are rewarded.

## The politician and escape

Netaji, a hugely fat corrupt politician (white kurta, rupee-note garland, walrus moustache, briefcase cuffed to his wrist, revolver), and Shera, his paid bodyguard: a giant mercenary about 1.4× CHAD's height (135 logical px) with rupee notes pinned to his vest like medals and a gold ₹ pendant (`js/train_neta.js`, two HUD bars: Shera 500, Netaji 300).

Phase 1, private carriage. Shera fights; Netaji hides behind him, lobs cash (a fetched bundle is a PAID buff) and fires a red revolver shot after a dotted aim line. Shera's deck: pound (two plain hooks, 8 each, into a green 22-damage hammer), a red bull rush (sidestep or jump; into a wall or luggage he crashes and staggers), a red leap quake and a red bear hug (a lunge that beats guard and parry: jump or change lane; a whiff leaves him stumbling with his back open, hits from behind pay 1.5×; a caught CHAD mashes free after the second squeeze). After a clean string he counts cash for 70 ticks (40 if hit). Palm catch: the third light in an opening (the second when UNPAID) is caught and answered with a 14-damage headbutt, so finish strings with a heavy. Only a parry riposte, a crash, a guard break, a reflected trunk or Netaji's stray bullet floors him (70 ticks); he gets up with a wake-up shove. Fetching and pocketing cash are armoured. Parry spam is penalised (TOO EARLY, 44-tick lock; an unused tap locks 26).

Phase 2, UNPAID: at 55% (or Netaji's bar at its floor, or 100 s), at a safe beat, Netaji shouts “HOLD HIM, SHERA!”, waddles to the ladder and climbs out mid-fight; his bar reads FLED. Shera turns, roars (a push) and fights red and torn-vested: faster, no count or PAID, a short roar breather every second string, lights between strings sting 30% without a flinch. His enraged deck adds a jump-only chain sweep and a luggage hurl (parry the trunk back for 30 and a crash stagger). Dying here restarts phase 2 at 45% with Netaji gone. At zero he stands out on his feet for 40 ticks, then `shera-finish` (`js/train_neta_cinematics.js`, 424 ticks): six gut punches, a rib crunch, a knee drop and super charge, a charged uppercut that severs his head (negative flash, neck spray, ceiling ricochet and floor bounce), a headless lift and trunk crash, a bloody aftermath and Duke's “visit to the dentist”; CHAD climbs the hatch ladder. Two commandos block the first roof section; at its end Netaji turns: “FINE. I'LL DO IT MYSELF.”

Roof duel: Netaji alone on a fresh 260 bar (scaled by difficulty). One 8-damage revolver shot per string after a 44-tick lane-tracking aim line (three rounds, then CLICK and a reload punish); a green 10-damage briefcase swing and pistol-whip, a red 5-damage grit throw, a green 5-damage shove (swing or shove replace grit when CHAD is close); and a kneeling bribe fake-out below 70% (hit him while he begs, or parry the flung cash back). He guards (2) only while backpedalling; lights on him standing pay 70%, and four flinches in a row trip him. The low gantry sweeps the far lanes; Netaji ducks under it. A roof death resumes the duel with the commandos skipped.
